whether the detector's own evidence is real
==========================================================================
called unfaithful                     4606
  quote is not in the summary          276     6.0% of flags
  refused to name any words at all      21     0.5% of flags

  ^ this is the number this half was built for. The prompt requires
    the model to quote the exact words it objects to, and that quote
    is checkable with no judgement at all: the string is in the
    summary or it is not.

    A detector that says 'unfaithful' and then quotes a phrase the
    summary never contained has invented its own evidence. Nothing
    else in this pipeline would have noticed, because the verdict
    would still have been scored as correct whenever it happened to
    land on an unfaithful summary.
